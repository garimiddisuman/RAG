# Thoughtworks STEP Program

> **Purpose:** This document is structured as a knowledge base for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It focuses on the Thoughtworks India **Software Technology Excellence Programme (STEP)**.
>
> **Important:** STEP information has changed over time. This document separates currently published information from historical information where possible. Current program facts should be taken from the current Thoughtworks STEP page.

---

## 1. What is STEP?

**STEP** stands for **Software Technology Excellence Programme**.

It is a Thoughtworks India entry-level programme designed around a **"work while you study"** model. Participants learn software engineering theory and practices while gaining practical experience through an internship at Thoughtworks.

The current Thoughtworks description presents STEP as an **18-month intensive entry-level programme**.

The programme combines:

- Software engineering education
- Practical software development experience
- Communication, language and personality-development courses
- Mentoring and exposure to experienced technologists
- A bachelor's degree programme funded by Thoughtworks
- A paid stipend
- A potential transition into a full-time Thoughtworks consultant role after successful completion

## 3. Why was STEP created?

Thoughtworks India historically identified a limitation in traditional technology hiring: relying heavily on engineering-college graduates could exclude talented people who did not have access to that educational path.

Thoughtworks began hiring interns from diploma colleges in India in **2011**.

The organization found that a conventional internship was not sufficient for candidates coming from vocational/diploma education because there could be a significant gap between academic education and practical software-engineering skills.

STEP was created to address this gap by combining:

1. Structured technical education
2. Practical software-development experience
3. Continuous learning
4. Professional development
5. Exposure to experienced Thoughtworkers

The intended outcome was to enable participants to become productive software professionals rather than remain traditional short-term interns.

---

## 4. STEP's core philosophy

The central idea of STEP is:

> **Learn theory and apply it while working.**

The programme creates a feedback loop between:

**Learning -> Practice -> Work -> Feedback -> Further Learning**

This is different from a model where a student completes education first and only then enters the software industry.

## 5. Current programme structure

According to the current Thoughtworks India STEP page, the programme is **18 months long**.

It combines four major components.

### 5.1 Study

Participants study more than 20 subjects related to software engineering.

The curriculum also includes:

- Communication
- Language
- Personality development

The goal is not only to develop programming ability but also broader professional capabilities.

### 5.2 Internship

Participants are based at a Thoughtworks office in India for their internship.

During the internship, they:

- Work alongside experienced Thoughtworkers
- Gain practical software-development exposure
- Learn how software is developed in a professional environment
- Apply concepts learned through the educational component

### 5.3 Degree

Thoughtworks funds the participant's enrolment in a **3-year Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)** programme.

The degree is provided through a government-recognised university using distance-education mode.

Important distinction:

- STEP itself is an 18-month Thoughtworks programme.
- The funded BCA is a separate 3-year academic programme associated with the STEP experience.

### 5.4 Employment

The current STEP page states that, after successful completion of the programme, participants can be absorbed by Thoughtworks as consultants.

The currently published minimum salary is:

**₹8 lakhs per annum CTC**

This should be treated as a current published figure and may change for future cohorts.

## 7. Eligibility

The current STEP eligibility criteria state that a candidate must:

1. Be in the final year of a **3-year Polytechnic Diploma**.
2. Complete final examinations before **1 July** of the year in which they apply.
3. Be **over 18 years old** on the programme start date.

### Important

The programme is intended for Polytechnic Diploma candidates.

The current page does not restrict eligibility to Computer Science diploma students.

Historically, Thoughtworks has explicitly described STEP as accepting diploma holders from different subjects, not only Computer Science.

## 8. Selection process

The current STEP page describes a campus-based selection process.

### Selection stages

1. **Written test**
2. **Personal interview – round 1**
3. **Personal interview – round 2**

The current page states that STEP selection is conducted through a **campus selection process**.

This is different from Thoughtworks' general graduate hiring process, which can involve recruiter screening, technical interviews, pairing and culture/leadership discussions.

## 19. STEP vs Thoughtworks University (TWU)

STEP and Thoughtworks University (TWU) are different programmes.

### STEP

Designed for:

- Polytechnic Diploma candidates
- Entry-level candidates
- Work-while-you-study development
- A structured pathway combining education, internship and degree

Current published duration:

**18 months**

### Thoughtworks University

TWU is a separate first-year learning programme for graduates and career changers.

The current Thoughtworks page describes TWU as a:

**One-year immersive training programme**

It begins with a four-week in-person learning experience in Pune and continues with structured learning, mentorship and hands-on client work.

Therefore:

```text

       |
       v
18-month work + study programme

Graduates / career changers
       |
       v
One-year first-year training programme
```

Do not treat STEP and TWU as interchangeable programmes.

## 22. STEP and diversity / access

Historically, Thoughtworks created STEP partly because conventional engineering-college hiring could create a diversity and access bottleneck.

The programme sought to provide opportunities to people who were capable of becoming strong software professionals but did not necessarily have access to traditional engineering-degree pathways.

This makes STEP part of Thoughtworks India's broader efforts around access and inclusion.
