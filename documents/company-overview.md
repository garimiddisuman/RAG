# Company Overview

## About the Company

Acme Technologies is a software company that builds cloud-based products for businesses.

The company was founded in 2021 and has engineering, product, design, sales, finance, and human resources teams.

## Working Hours

The standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to be available during core hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Teams

The Engineering organization consists of Backend, Frontend, Platform, QA, DevOps, and Security teams.

The Engineering team reports to the VP of Engineering.

## Office Locations

Acme Technologies currently has offices in Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

Employees working remotely should follow the company's remote work policy.
