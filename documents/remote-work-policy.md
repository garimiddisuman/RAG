# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period are eligible for remote work unless their role requires regular physical presence.

Managers may approve remote work requests based on team requirements.

## Remote Working Days

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three days per week.

Employees should work from a location with a reliable internet connection.

## Availability

Remote employees must remain available during core working hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Employees should keep their company communication tools active during working hours.

## Equipment

The company provides employees with a laptop for work purposes.

Employees are responsible for taking reasonable care of company equipment while working remotely.
