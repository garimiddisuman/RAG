# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance

Full-time employees are eligible for company-provided health insurance.

The insurance plan covers the employee and eligible dependents according to the current insurance policy.

## Internet Allowance

Employees who regularly work remotely may be eligible for a monthly internet allowance.

The allowance is subject to the company's current benefits policy.

## Learning Budget

Employees receive an annual learning budget of ₹25,000.

The budget can be used for approved technical courses, conferences, books, and professional certifications.

Employees must obtain manager approval before using the learning budget.

## Equipment

Engineering employees are provided with a company laptop.

Additional equipment may be provided when required for the employee's role and approved by the manager.
