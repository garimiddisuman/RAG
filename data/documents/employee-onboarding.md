# Employee Onboarding

## First Day

New employees should report to their assigned office or connect remotely by 9:30 AM on their first working day.

The HR team will provide the employee with their laptop, employee ID, and onboarding instructions.

## Account Setup

Every employee receives a company email account.

Engineering employees will also receive access to GitHub, Slack, Jira, and the internal development systems.

Access requests should be submitted through the IT service desk.

## Onboarding Training

All new employees must complete the following training during their first two weeks:

- Company introduction
- Security awareness
- Code of conduct
- Data protection
- Development environment setup

## Probation

New employees have a six-month probation period.

The employee's manager conducts a probation review before the end of the probation period.
