# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave for every calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance.

Employees should request leave through the company's HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

If an employee is absent for more than two consecutive working days because of illness, supporting medical documentation may be requested.

## Carry Forward

Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following calendar year.

Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Public Holidays

The company observes the public holidays published in the annual holiday calendar.

The holiday calendar is maintained by the HR team.
